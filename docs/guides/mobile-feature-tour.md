These are source features, not a claim that every provider or service was exercised. The demo messages were seeded, the approval was not executed, and the routine is disabled. Widget and Live Activity screens show built-in sample previews. Connector access requires account sign-in; the marketplace screenshot shows that requirement. Cloud setup is not proof of a provisioned workspace. No account credentials or customer content were copied.

The capture-only navigation and demo pairing were confined to a temporary build, leaving production authentication intact. Installed TestFlight build 30 does not contain these later welcome-color changes; a new build is required to see them on a physical phone.
